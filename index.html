Atlas of the Offshore World: Effective Tax Rates on Labor, Capital and Corporations

With Pierre Bachas · International Tax Observatory, Paris School of Economics · Database · 2026

A new cross-country database of effective tax rates for more than 150 countries from 1965 to 2023. This is an effort by the International Tax Observatory and Skatteforsk to provide a consistent and comparable set of effective tax rates on labor, capital and corporations across countries and over time. I harmonized records from the OECD, IMF, ICTD, World Inequality Database and national tax authorities into consistent series that allow tax systems to be compared across regions and levels of development.

Policy

Progressive Taxation and Inequality: Minimum Taxation of High-Net-Worth Individuals in Latin America and the Caribbean

With Vicente Silva, Quentin Parrinello, Lamia Oualalou, Carmen Durrer de La Sota, Juan Vergara and Gabriel Zucman · International Tax Observatory, Paris School of Economics · Policy report · 2026

Commissioned by Brazil under its Presidency of the Regional Platform for Tax Cooperation for Latin America and the Caribbean (PTLAC), this report analyses the progressivity of tax systems in the region and their limits in taxing very high-net-worth individuals. We show that tax systems in Latin America are broadly regressive: the poorest 50% pay around 30% of their income in taxes, while the richest 1% pay around 22%, and the richest 0.01% pay as little as 20% in Brazil and 11% in Chile. After evaluating several reform options, we find that a minimum wealth tax, a floor that charges the difference when taxes already paid fall below a threshold, is the most effective instrument. For Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Mexico, Peru and Uruguay, a 2% minimum tax on fortunes above USD 100 million would raise about USD 24 billion a year (0.6% of GDP), and a 3% rate about USD 36 billion (0.9% of GDP)

African Tax Outlook 2025, Section 7.2: Effective Tax Rates in Africa

With Noé Manuel Mendoza · African Tax Administration Forum, International Tax Observatory and Skatteforsk · Policy report contribution · 2025

I contributed to Section 7.2, "Effective Tax Rates in Africa", of the 2025 African Tax Outlook, the flagship publication of the African Tax Administration Forum, which analyses tax and non-tax revenues and revenue administration data for 39 African countries. Using data from the Atlas of the Offshore World, I produced the figures and co-wrote the analysis of effective tax rates on capital and labor for Africa and its five sub-regions between 2011 and 2021. The section shows that Africa's effective tax rates are among the lowest in the world and have not improved in a decade, that low labor rates mainly reflect informality rather than low statutory rates, and it makes the case for distributional analysis of who bears the tax burden.

Tax for SDGs Initiative

United Nations Development Programme, Honduras · Policy work · 2024

The Tax for SDGs Initiative supports developing countries in mobilizing domestic revenue and using tax policy to advance the Sustainable Development Goals. In Honduras, I coordinated the initiative with the national tax authority (SAR) and the Ministry of Finance, including the SDGs Taxation Framework working sessions with government officials. I also worked on sovereign debt management, looking at how fiscal governance and tax administration capacity relate to a country's access to international financial markets.

Master's thesis

Do U.S. Presidential Elections Have an Impact on Deportation Patterns?

Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne · 2021

Using a state-level panel for 2004 to 2019 with time fixed effects, I study whether vote shares in the 2004, 2008, 2012 and 2016 U.S. presidential elections are associated with the composition of deportations. A higher Democratic vote share is associated with a lower share of criminal, non-conviction and total deportations, while a higher Republican vote share is associated with an increase in all three.
