I

About

I am a Honduran economist based in France. From 2024 to 2026 I was a pre-doctoral researcher at the International Tax Observatory at the Paris School of Economics, where I built and harmonized cross-country data on effective tax rates and worked on policy research on the taxation of high-net-worth individuals in Latin America.

Before that, I worked as a consultant economist with the United Nations Development Programme in Honduras, coordinating the Tax for SDGs Initiative with the national tax authority and working on sovereign debt management with the Ministry of Finance. I started my career as a financial analyst at Banco Davivienda, where four years of risk analysis on multinational and local firms first got me interested in how firms respond to tax and policy environments.

I hold a research-track Master's in Development Economics from Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne and a Bachelor's in Finance from Universidad Católica de Honduras. I work in Spanish, English and French.

III

Research

Database · 2026

Atlas of the Offshore World: Effective Tax Rates on Labor, Capital and Corporations

With Pierre Bachas

A cross-country database of effective tax rates for more than 150 countries from 1965 to 2023. I harmonized records from the OECD, the IMF, the ICTD, the World Inequality Database and national tax authorities into a consistent series that allows tax systems to be compared across regions and levels of development.

Policy report · 2026

Progressive Taxation and Inequality: Minimum Taxation of High-Net-Worth Individuals in Latin America and the Caribbean

With V. Silva, Q. Parrinello, C. Durrer, J. Vergara and G. Zucman

An empirical assessment of minimum wealth taxation across seven Latin American countries. We combine household wealth surveys, administrative fiscal records and tax microsimulation models to estimate the revenue potential and distributional effects of a 2 to 3 percent minimum tax on fortunes above 100 million to 1 billion US dollars.
